What We Do

Our programmes respond to the needs of vulnerable people and communities.

DRIMORIA SUPPORT ORGANISATION works through a range of community-focused programmes designed to empower vulnerable people, promote education, protect children and create opportunities for self-reliance.

Our programmes focus on girls, women, youth, orphans, street children and the wider community. We believe that sustainable change comes from combining support with education, skills development, participation and opportunity.

YOUTH EMPOWERMENT

Investing in the Next Generation

Young people have an important role to play in building stronger communities. DRIMORIA seeks to create spaces where youth can learn, develop practical skills, build confidence and participate positively in their communities.

Through mentorship, education, skills development, leadership and community participation, we aim to help young people discover their potential and work towards self-reliance.
