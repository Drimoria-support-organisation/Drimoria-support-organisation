Our Programs

DRIMORIA operates six core programs to address the diverse needs of vulnerable populations in our community.

👩‍🎓

Women Empowerment

Economic and social empowerment programs for girls and women including vocational training and business support. We provide skills training, microfinance support, and mentorship to help women become economically independent.

🏫

Education for Orphans

Comprehensive educational support, scholarships, and mentoring for orphaned children to achieve their potential. We cover school fees, provide learning materials, and offer scholarly mentorship.

🏠

Street Children Care

Safe shelter, rehabilitation, healthcare, and reintegration support for street children and youth. We provide housing, medical care, counseling, and family reintegration services.

🤝

Community Development

Initiatives promoting self-reliance, community awareness, and sustainable development in local areas. We organize training, community projects, and awareness campaigns.

⚖️

Rights Advocacy

Advocacy for protection of vulnerable people's rights and raising community awareness on social issues. We champion policy changes and community education.

🌍

Partnership & Welfare

Collaborating with partners to improve social welfare systems and create positive community impact. We work with governments, NGOs, and international organizations.
