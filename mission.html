Mission & Vision

The purpose and direction behind our work

🎯

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable girls, women and youth, support orphans, and provide care, protection and accommodation to street children through sustainable community initiatives, education, skills development and opportunities for self-reliance.

🌍

Our Vision

To build a compassionate, empowered and inclusive community where girls, women, youth, orphans and street children live safe, dignified and productive lives, with young people equipped to become responsible leaders and positive agents of change.

OUR COMMITMENT TO YOUTH

Young People Are Part of the Future

DRIMORIA believes that young people are an important part of building stronger and more sustainable communities. We are committed to creating opportunities for youth through education, mentorship, skills development, leadership training and community participation.

We aim to help young people discover their potential, develop practical skills, become self-reliant and participate positively in the development of their communities.

Our Objectives

Key areas through which DRIMORIA seeks to create positive change.

01

Empower Vulnerable People

Support girls, women, youth, orphans and street children with opportunities that promote dignity and self-reliance.

02

Promote Education

Encourage access to education and learning opportunities for vulnerable children and young people.

03

Develop Practical Skills

Promote skills development that can help individuals become more capable and economically self-reliant.

04

Strengthen Youth Leadership

Encourage young people to develop leadership skills and participate positively in community development.

05

Protect Vulnerable Children

Promote care, protection and safe environments for orphans and vulnerable street children.

06

Build Stronger Communities

Support sustainable initiatives that encourage community participation, cooperation and positive social change.
