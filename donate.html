Support DRIMORIA

Your support can help restore hope and create opportunity.

Why Your Support Matters

Every contribution can help us continue serving vulnerable people and communities.

DRIMORIA SUPPORT ORGANISATION works to empower vulnerable girls, women and youth, support orphans, provide care and protection for street children, and strengthen communities.

Financial support helps us continue developing and supporting initiatives in areas such as education, skills development, youth empowerment, child support, community development and welfare.

Whether your contribution is large or small, your support can contribute to creating opportunities and improving the lives of people who need support.

What Your Support Can Help With

Support can contribute to different areas of our work.

📚

Education

Supporting educational opportunities for orphans, vulnerable children and young people.

🎓

Youth Development

Supporting mentorship, skills development, leadership and opportunities for young people.

👩

Women Empowerment

Supporting initiatives that help women develop skills and work towards greater self-reliance.

❤️

Child Support

Helping provide care, protection and support for vulnerable children.

🌱

Community Development

Supporting initiatives that encourage participation, cooperation and sustainable community development.

🤝

Welfare Support

Contributing to welfare and community support initiatives for people facing difficult circumstances.
