About Us

DRIMORIA SUPPORT ORGANISATION is a dedicated charity focused on uplifting vulnerable girls and women, supporting orphans, and providing comprehensive care to street children. Through sustainable community initiatives, we work to create lasting change in the lives of the most vulnerable members of our society.

Since our inception, we have been committed to providing not just immediate relief, but long-term solutions that empower individuals and strengthen communities.

Our organization operates with transparency, integrity, and a deep commitment to making a measurable difference. We believe that every child deserves a safe place to grow, every woman deserves the opportunity to thrive, and every community deserves a brighter future.

We work closely with local partners, government agencies, and international organizations to amplify our impact and ensure sustainable development in the communities we serve.
